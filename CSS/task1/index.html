Parliament Approves Landmark Digital Infrastructure Bill

By Tech Desk | Updated 20 minutes ago

In a historic vote late Monday evening, Parliament passed the Digital Infrastructure Modernization Bill, paving the way for upgraded fiber-optic networks across rural districts and enforcing strict data sovereignty standards.

The bill received bipartisan support following intense debates on privacy safeguards. Key industry leaders have praised the decision, noting that it establishes a solid framework for cloud infrastructure and tech innovation across the region over the next decade.
